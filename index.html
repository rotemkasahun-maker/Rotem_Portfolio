FEATURED CASE STUDY · IN PROGRESS

Family Finance

It started at home: where is our money actually going?

The data was scattered across receipts, SMS, email, cards and payment apps.

Then the deeper problem appeared: the system depended on remembering.

The key was to remove remembering from the workflow wherever possible.

Automation solved collection. It did not automatically solve motivation.

So the product evolved toward proactive support, personalization and gamification.

SMS → Detection → Normalize → Queue → Sync → Financial evidence

MVP in active development.
