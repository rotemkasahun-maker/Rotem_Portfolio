FEATURED CASE STUDY · IN PROGRESS

Family Finance

A family-finance system designed around real behavior, not perfect discipline.

It started with one question: where is our money actually going?

The information was scattered across SMS, email, receipts, cards and payment apps.

Then the deeper problem appeared: the system depended on remembering.

The key was to remove remembering from the workflow wherever possible.

THE FRICTION

Manual tracking creates repeated cognitive work.

THE DIRECTION

Collect automatically. Surface only what still needs attention.

SMS→Detect→Normalize→Queue→Sync
PRODUCT LEARNING

Automation solved collection. It didn’t solve motivation.

That led to proactive support, personalization and a gamification hypothesis around meaningful goals.
