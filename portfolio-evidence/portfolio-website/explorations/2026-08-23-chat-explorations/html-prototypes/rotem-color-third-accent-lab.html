ONLY THE THIRD ACCENT CHANGES

What replaces
the blue?

Watermelon and the fresh green are fixed. The background, shadows, overlap and A-style section division are also fixed. We are only testing the third-color role — including the option of not having a third hue at all.

Q · NO THIRD HUE

Follow what people actually do.

Ivory + watermelon + fresh green only. Depth comes from lighter and darker variants instead of adding another color family.

The evidence existed. The collection was the work.

01
Fewer colors

The palette feels cohesive because the system has only two expressive hues.

02
More tonal depth
