Depth & Materiality Lab · color intentionally secondary

What should the site feel made of?

D was useful because it had weight. This round isolates the missing dimension: physicality, layering and atmosphere. Same calm philosophy; four structurally different kinds of depth.

01 · Layered papertactile / imperfect / warm
Material direction

Things feel placed, not rendered.

Evidence behaves like material on a desk: overlapping sheets, a little tape, slight rotation and shadows that are not perfectly uniform.

a little human, on purpose
Potential strength: screenshots can become physical evidence without turning every section into a card.
02 · Atmospheric depthsoft / immersive / breezy
Material direction

Depth without visible furniture.

Instead of stacks of cards, the page has atmosphere: blurred color masses, translucent surfaces and shadows that suggest distance.

Potential strength: especially compatible with subtle scroll parallax and the “breeze” motion language.
03 · Editorial collisionartifact-led / asymmetrical / composed
Material direction

The evidence is allowed to interrupt the layout.

Large artifacts cross the composition, captions sit partly outside them, and depth comes from overlap rather than decorative containers.

A caption can sit where the observation happened — not where a component library expects it.
Potential strength: gives case studies visual authority while keeping the interface itself restrained.
04 · Graphic physicalweight / quirk / controlled imperfection
Material direction

Clean structure. Slightly unruly objects.

The stronger weight you liked in D stays, but geometry becomes less perfect. Offset shadows and overlaps make elements feel like they have mass.

Everything knows the grid.
Not everything obeys it.
Potential strength: enough static personality that motion can stay gentle and reserve its bigger gestures for rare kicks.
