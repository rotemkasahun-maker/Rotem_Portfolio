ROTEM PORTFOLIO · COLOR LAB 08 — six genuinely different brand worlds
WIDE EXPLORATION

Not tweaks.
Different personalities.

The A-style visual system stays locked: warm light ground, layered cards, real shadows, overlap, editorial section breaks and generous whitespace. Everything below explores a different color identity. The apricot direction from the previous round remains a reference, but is not repeated as an option.

R · TANGERINE + LAVENDER

Follow what people actually do.

Tangerine brings immediate warmth and joy; lavender makes it creative rather than summery or food-like.

The evidence existed. The collection was the work.

01
Warm confidence

Orange is allowed to be clean and cheerful rather than earthy.

02
Creative counterpoint

Lavender prevents the warmth from feeling one-note.

Feel: joyful · inventive · sunny · contemporary
S · CHERRY + PINK + MINT

Follow what people actually do.

Graphic cherry red, soft pink and clean mint. More fashion/editorial and energetic, with plenty of cream keeping it grown-up.

The evidence existed. The collection was the work.

01
Graphic energy

Cherry gives a sharper visual signature than dusty coral.

02
Fresh relief

Mint breaks the warm family without turning corporate.

Feel: graphic · joyful · editorial · personable
T · VIOLET + PEACH + BUTTER

Follow what people actually do.

A confident violet family paired with peach and a pale buttery neutral. Stronger cool/warm contrast without relying on blue.

The evidence existed. The collection was the work.

01
Distinct identity

Violet feels designed and expressive rather than conventionally corporate.

02
Warm balance

Peach keeps the page welcoming and human.

Feel: bold · creative · warm · memorable
U · PISTACHIO + RASPBERRY

Follow what people actually do.

Light pistachio leads, with raspberry and apricot as expressive accents. Fresh and happy without reading as nature branding.

The evidence existed. The collection was the work.

01
Fresh base

Pistachio is brighter and more playful than sage.

02
Raspberry punch

A small amount of richer pink gives it personality.

Feel: fresh · happy · quirky · warm
V · TOMATO + LILAC + AQUA

Follow what people actually do.

Tomato red brings energy, while lilac and pale aqua make the combination feel contemporary and unexpected.

The evidence existed. The collection was the work.

01

Tomato is warm and alive without becoming fluorescent.

02
Unexpected softness

W · PEACH + BURGUNDY + MINT

Follow what people actually do.

Peach provides joy and lightness; burgundy gives adult depth; mint stops the warm tones from becoming too sweet.

The evidence existed. The collection was the work.

02
