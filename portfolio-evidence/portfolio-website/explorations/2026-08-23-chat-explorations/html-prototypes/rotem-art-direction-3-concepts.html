HOW TO REACT TO THESE

You can mix ingredients.

01 · Desire Path Editorial

Best if you like dramatic typography, asymmetry, large evidence moments and the desire-path idea becoming the site's structural metaphor.

02 · Living Research Notebook

Best if you like visible thinking, annotations, decision logs and a more tactile/human feeling — without turning into scrapbook aesthetics.

03 · Playful Product System

Best if you want the site to feel most digital and interactive, with motion and product-system behavior carrying more of the personality.
