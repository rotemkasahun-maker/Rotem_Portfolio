THE FRICTION

The information existed. The system depended on remembering to collect it.

01 · Context

One question exposed a fragmented system.

It started with a simple question: where is our money actually going?

The information was scattered across SMS, email, receipts, cards and payment apps. Seeing the whole picture meant repeatedly collecting, interpreting and categorizing evidence from different places.

02 · Observation

Manual tracking creates repeated cognitive work.

The deeper problem was not only missing data. The workflow relied on someone remembering to gather it, move it and keep it current.

Remove remembering from the workflow wherever possible.

THE DIRECTION

Collect automatically.
Surface only what still needs attention.

The initial technical flow focuses on turning incoming financial evidence into a normalized queue that can be synced instead of manually reconstructed later.

SMS→Detect→Normalize→Queue→Sync

WHAT EXISTS TODAY

Not a concept deck. A working ingestion path.

The current Android build detects financial SMS candidates, normalizes evidence, persists a local queue and exposes sync status. The product is still evolving, so this section separates what is built from what is still a hypothesis.

BUILTCandidate detection

Incoming SMS can be screened for likely financial evidence.

BUILTNormalized evidence

Different messages can enter one consistent processing shape.

BUILTLocal queue

Pending evidence survives locally instead of depending on memory.

IN PROGRESSUseful support

The next question is how collected data becomes help people actually care about.

03 · Product learning

Automation solved collection. It didn’t solve motivation.

The work shifted from “how do we capture data?” toward what happens after the system has it: proactive support, personalization and a hypothesis around meaningful goals.

This is an active project. The direction above reflects the current product learning; it is not presented as a finished outcome.
